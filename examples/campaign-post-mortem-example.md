# Campaign Post-Mortem Example

## Prompt

```text
/campaign-post-mortem Our spring paid social campaign ran 1-30 April with 50k GBP spend. Goal was profitable subscriber acquisition. We have spend, impressions, clicks, signups, CAC, first-month discount, plan type, and early churn.
```

## Example Output

## Campaign Post-Mortem: Spring Paid Social Subscriber Acquisition

### Executive Summary

The campaign delivered subscriber volume, but profitability depends on early churn and discount dependency. Platform-level acquisition looked efficient only if first-month signups retain at expected rates. The next decision should be based on channel-specific retention and margin-adjusted payback, not signup volume alone.

### Campaign Context

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Objective | Acquire profitable subscribers |
| Dates | 1-30 April |
| Channels | Paid social |
| Audience | Prospecting and retargeting |
| Budget | 50k GBP |

### Performance Scorecard

| Metric | Target/Baseline | Actual | Readout |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Spend | 50k GBP | 50k GBP | Fully delivered |
| CAC | 45 GBP | 42 GBP | Better than target |
| Signups | 1,100 | 1,190 | Above target |
| First-month discount cost | 8 GBP | 9 GBP | Slightly worse than plan |
| Early churn | 7% | 10% | Quality concern |

### Driver Analysis

| Driver | What happened | Evidence | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Delivery | Budget spent as planned | Spend reached 50k GBP | High |
| Acquisition efficiency | CAC beat target | 42 GBP actual CAC vs 45 GBP target | High |
| Customer quality | Early churn rose | 10% early churn vs 7% expectation | Medium |
| Offer economics | Discount cost was higher than planned | 9 GBP vs 8 GBP | Medium |
| Measurement | Incrementality is unproven | No holdout or comparison group noted | High |

### What Worked

- Campaign delivered volume at a CAC below target.
- Signup count exceeded plan.
- Spend delivery was stable.

### What Did Not Work

- Early churn suggests some acquired subscribers may be lower quality.
- Discount costs were slightly higher than planned.
- The campaign cannot yet be described as incremental without a comparison group.

### Caveats and Measurement Limits

- Paid social signups are attributed, not necessarily incremental.
- Early churn may change as cohorts mature.
- Margin and payback need plan-level revenue and fulfilment cost.

### Recommendations

- Split results by campaign, creative, audience, and plan type.
- Compare 90-day retention for paid social subscribers against organic and email-acquired cohorts.
- Cap budget increases until margin-adjusted payback is confirmed.
- Test a lower-discount creative or higher-intent audience to improve quality.

### Next Test or Analysis

Run a channel-quality analysis comparing paid social cohorts against comparable non-paid cohorts on 90-day retention, plan value, discount cost, and gross margin.
